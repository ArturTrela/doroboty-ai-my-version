# Real-person prototype test

Use a synthetic persona and example CV. Explain that the session tests the interface, not the person or their skills. Obtain consent and record only a synthetic participant code, date and prototype version.

Ask the tester to find a relevant role, explain the employer need, prepare/edit an application, and submit it. Observe what they expect at each step without teaching the interface. In a separate task, switch to the employer and inspect the application.

Do not treat fixed Match Scores or simulated AI output as evidence of recruiting effectiveness. Record observed behavior, surprise, limitations, decision and next test in docs/learnings, without real personal details.
